# FORGE — Product Overview

**Built by Intelligent Systems · By Africa, for Africa**

FORGE is a marketplace that connects people who need skilled hands with blue-collar professionals who do the work — electricians, plumbers, carpenters, builders, and more. It is built for everyday life in **Ghana, Nigeria, and Togo**: find someone you can trust, hire them, manage the job, and get paid (or pay) with more confidence than a random number from WhatsApp.

Think of FORGE as two sides of one platform:

- **Customer side** — “I need help. Find me the right pro.”
- **Worker side** — “I have skills. Help me get real work.”

---

## The problem FORGE solves

When something breaks at home or on a project, people often:

- Ask friends for a number
- Gamble on a stranger
- Struggle to know who is skilled, available, or trustworthy

Meanwhile, skilled workers often:

- Wait for referrals
- Struggle to be discovered
- Lose work to informal, untracked deals

FORGE turns that into a clear loop: **discover → hire → work → pay → review**.

## Main features (and why they matter)

### 1. Dual experience (Customer OS & Worker OS)

The app feels different depending on who you are.

- **Customers** see hiring tools: find pros, my jobs, bookings, favorites.
- **Workers** see business tools: job feed, applications, bookings, portfolio, wallet, dashboard.

**Benefit:** Each person gets a focused experience — not a cluttered one-size-fits-all app.

---

### 2. Sign up & sign in (including Google)

Users create an account as a **customer** or **worker**, or continue with Google.

**Benefit:** Fast start. Workers go into onboarding; customers can begin hiring sooner.

---

### 3. Worker onboarding

New workers complete a guided setup (profile, skills, basic business info) before fully using the worker side.

**Benefit:** Pros show up ready to be hired — not as empty profiles.

---

### 4. Find pros / worker search

Customers browse and filter skilled workers by location, skills, rates, and other hiring signals.

**Benefit:** Less guesswork. You search with intent instead of hoping a friend has “someone.”

---

### 5. Worker profiles

Each pro has a public-facing profile with name, skills, bio, location, ratings, and related details.

**Benefit:** Customers can judge fit before messaging or booking — transparency builds trust.

---

### 6. Portfolio & endorsements

Workers can showcase past work and social proof from people who know their craft.

**Benefit:** Proof of skill, not just claims. Helps good workers stand out.

---

### 7. Post projects (jobs)

Customers can post work they need done so workers can discover and respond.

**Benefit:** You don’t only browse people — you can broadcast the job and let the right hands find you.

---

### 8. Job applications

Workers can apply to open projects in a structured way (not only informal chat).

**Benefit:** Clearer pipeline for both sides: “who applied,” “who’s interested,” “what’s next.”

---

### 9. Bookings

The heart of hiring: request a booking, accept/manage it, move work through statuses (pending → accepted → in progress → completed → reviewed).

**Benefit:** The job has a lifecycle. Everyone knows where things stand instead of chasing updates on scattered chats.

---

### 10. Favorites (saved workers)

Customers can save pros they trust for future work.

**Benefit:** Faster rehiring. When the next pipe bursts, your shortlist is already there.

---

### 11. Messaging / chat

Customers and workers message inside the app around jobs, bookings, and profiles.

**Benefit:** One place for conversation tied to the work — less “lost in WhatsApp,” more accountability.

---

### 12. Notifications

In-app alerts for important activity (messages, booking updates, and related events).

**Benefit:** You don’t miss the moment that matters — acceptance, a new message, a status change.

---

### 13. Reviews & ratings

After completed work, customers can leave ratings and feedback that show on worker profiles.

**Benefit:** Trust compounds. Good work is rewarded with reputation; bad experiences become visible.

---

### 14. Verification / KYC uploads

Workers can submit identity or verification documents for platform review (admin-controlled approval).

**Benefit:** Stronger trust signal. Customers gain more confidence that a pro is who they say they are.

---

### 15. Subscriptions (worker visibility)

Workers can upgrade plans (via Paystack) to improve visibility and marketplace presence.

**Benefit:** Serious pros can invest in growth; the platform can sustainably surface committed workers.

---

### 16. Payments & escrow foundations

Booking payment can be held more safely (escrow-style hold → release when work completes, or refund when cancelled appropriately). Powered with Paystack for real-money flows in the region.

**Benefit:** Customers feel safer paying; workers have a clearer path to getting paid for completed work.

---

### 17. Wallet & earnings view

Workers get a wallet/ledger view of balances and money movement related to platform activity.

**Benefit:** Business clarity — what you’re owed, what’s pending, what’s happening with payouts over time.

*(Bank withdrawals / full payout automation can still expand further as the product matures.)*

---

### 18. AI matching & guidance

An AI assistant helps with matching and practical guidance (who might fit, how to think about the hire).

**Benefit:** Faster decisions for customers; less overwhelm when you don’t know which trade or pro to start with.

---

### 19. Disputes

When something goes wrong, parties can open a dispute for platform handling instead of fighting in silence.

**Benefit:** A safety valve for trust. Conflict has a process, not only an argument.

---

### 20. Customer & worker dashboards

Each role has a home hub for their pipeline (hires in progress, requests, applications, key actions).

**Benefit:** At-a-glance control of your side of the marketplace.

---

### 21. Admin tools

Admins can oversee platform health (including verification review and operational oversight).

**Benefit:** The marketplace can be moderated and kept trustworthy as it grows.

---

### 22. Mobile-first / installable experience (PWA)

FORGE works as a modern web app that feels app-like on phones — important for how people actually hire and hustle in the region.

**Benefit:** No heavy app-store barrier. Open the link, use it, come back easily.

---

### 23. Multi-country support

Designed around markets like **Ghana, Nigeria, and Togo** (local context, hiring reality, regional growth).

**Benefit:** Built for African labour markets first — not a generic global template with Africa as an afterthought.

---

## How a typical hire works (simple story)

1. A customer has a problem (leak, wiring, renovation, etc.).
2. They search FORGE or post a project.
3. They review a worker’s profile, portfolio, and ratings.
4. They book (or workers apply to the posted job).
5. Both sides chat and agree on details.
6. Work happens; status updates keep everyone aligned.
7. Payment is handled more safely through the platform’s payment flow.
8. The customer leaves a review.
9. Next time, the customer can rehire from favorites.
